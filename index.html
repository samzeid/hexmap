Dungeon Crawling in Nox

Exploring dungeons in Nox is measured in minutes, players interact with rooms in their entirety through. Skill checks while dungeon crawling may be made by the DM so that the result is hidden from players.

Room Entry

When the party enters into a new area, each character chooses an approach before resolving any Room Actions.

ApproachEffect

Settlement Services

During downtime, as a downtime activity you can access the following services. These services aren't always available, depending on the size of the settlement, the availability of skilled labour, or just bad luck.

Storage and Transport Services

You can rent storage in a settlement and pay for couriering between towns. Though travel paths are sometimes treacherous or not supported.

Hireling Services

Hiring a skilled commoner, (commoner stat block), requires locating them through a successful research or network downtime activity. Once hired they expect:

  • A minimum of 3 gp/day.

If taken outside of their settlement:

  • An additional 3gp/day.
  • Food, shelter, and safe transport for the duration of their service, including the return trip.

A hireling's interests are self-serving, they are unlikely to put up with poor treatment for long.

More specialised hirelings are typically handled through an open quest, or seal wall post. Which will require negotiation with the DM.

Messaging Services

Messages can be sent between settlements by raven for 5 gold pieces per parchment. The price includes the bird, the message tube, a wax seal, and notification of the contact. Delivery time varies by distance and weather, but most arrive within 1–3 days.

Exotic Metals

Silvered Weapon

Undead with immunity or resistance to nonmagical weapons are susceptible to silvered ones. Additionally, vampires and shapechangers are vulnerable to a silvered weapon. Silvering a weapon or 10 pieces of ammunition costs 100 gp.

Adamantine and Mithral

Adamantine and mithral are not magical, but ignore effects that corrode items. An experienced smith can forge adamantine or mithral into armor, a weapon, or 10 pieces of ammunition, for an additional 500gp.

Mithral Weapon

When you roll heroic inspiration for a mithral weapon attack, you do so with advantage.

Mithral Armor

If the armor normally imposes disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks or has a Strength requirement, the mithral version of the armor doesn't.

Adamantine Weapon

When an adamantine weapon or piece of ammunition hits an object, the hit is a critical hit.

Adamantine Armor

While you're wearing adamantine armor, any critical hit against you becomes a normal hit.

Hex Crawling in Nox

Travel is measured in 9-mile hexes. Each hex consists of a terrain type and may contain additional elements of the following; hazards, creatures, or locations (such as ruins, lairs, settlements, etc.)

Each element can be either overt or concealed. Overt elements can be revealed with the Survey Hex Action. Concealed elements require the Track or Explore Hex Actions to be interacted with after entering the hex.

Movement

The party gains 6 travel points per day on foot, or if all are mounted, travel points as listed in the Mount Travel Points table. Each terrain type has a different travel point cost to traverse. Additionally, you can adjust your movement in one of the following ways:

  • Move cautiously. Spend 3 travel points; the party gains +5 to all skill checks or saving throws made.
  • Forced march. Forgo rest to gain 3 travel points.

Hazards

Hazards reflect dangerous terrain, enemy presence, or environmental threats. To avoid a hazard's effects, the party must succeed on a group check of the skill or saving throw listed in the Hex Hazards Table.

Weather. Extreme weather conditions increase the terrain type's difficulty by one step. Severe weather may also introduce additional hazards in affected hexes.

Rations and Rest

Each creature consumes 1 ration per day of travel.

  • A short rest takes 8 hours and fulfills sleep requirements for the day.
  • A long rest requires 7 days of downtime, which must take place in a town, city, or secured location. You regain all your hit die when you finish a long rest.

Deprivation. Each day a creature goes without either food or sleep, it must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or gain one level of exhaustion. If the creature goes without both, increase the DC by 5. For every two consecutive days of deprivation, increase the DC by an additional 5.

Hex Actions

Each party member can perform one action from the Hex Actions table over the day. To do so, make the associated skill check against the terrain's DC (or the concealment DC). At the end of a travel day, one party member can perform an additional Camp action unless they made a forced march.

Inhospitable. Some hexes may not allow certain hex actions due to their inhospitable nature. When this is the case, it is an overt feature of the hex.

Exhaustion in Nox

While you have the Exhaustion condition, you experience the following effects.

  • Exhaustion Levels. This condition is cumulative. Each time you receive it, you gain 1 Exhaustion level. If your Exhaustion level reaches 6, you die.
  • D20 Tests Affected. When you make a D20 Test, the roll is reduced by your exhaustion level.
  • Spell Save DC Affected. Subtract your exhaustion level from the Spell save DC of any Spell you cast.
  • Speed Reduced. Your speed is reduced by 5 feet for every 2 levels of Exhaustion you have.
  • Removing Exhaustion Levels. A long rest reduces your Exhaustion level by 1. When your Exhaustion level reaches 0, the condition ends.
Fall Damage in Nox
  • Fall Damage. When you fall, you take 1d6 damage for every 5 feet fallen and land prone. If you willingly jump or drop, ignore the first 10 feet of the fall.
  • Saving Throw. You can make a Dexterity (Acrobatics) check with a DC equal to the number of feet fallen beyond any ignored distance. On a success, reduce the fall by an additional 10 feet and land standing instead of prone.
